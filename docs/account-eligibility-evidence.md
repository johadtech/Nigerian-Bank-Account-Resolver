# Account-Eligibility Evidence for Missing CBN Institutions

Retrieved and reviewed during the 2026-08-25 resolver audit. An institution is eligible for resolver inclusion only when an official source indicates that customers receive an account or wallet and can send or receive money through it. This evidence does not, by itself, verify a transfer code, NUBAN prefix, or account-number rule.

## Verified candidates

| Institution | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| SunTrust Bank Nigeria | Official website advertises personal, current, savings, and SME accounts, including opening an account online. | https://suntrustng.com/ |
| Hope Payment Service Bank | Official website says customers can open a personal account, transfer or receive money by phone number, and send money to other bank accounts. | https://hopepsbank.com/ |
| SmartCash Payment Service Bank | Official website says users can send money to any bank account and receive money from any bank in Nigeria into a SmartCash wallet. | https://smartcashpsb.ng/ |

| 9 Payment Service Bank (9PSB) | Official FAQ states that a customer's phone number is also their account number and that it follows a NUBAN standard; the official site documents Bank9ja transfers and account onboarding. | https://9psb.com.ng/faq/ and https://9psb.com.ng/bank9ja/ |
| MoMo Payment Service Bank | Official website says users can open a MoMo account, send and receive money, transfer from a bank to a MoMo wallet, and generate statements. | https://www.momo.ng/ |

## Evidence still required

PremiumTrust Bank's homepage was protected by a verification page during extraction, so no account-eligibility claim is promoted from that page yet. First Bank Nigeria, Nova Commercial Bank, Standard Chartered Bank Nigeria, and Titan Trust Bank still require direct official account-product evidence in this audit. The resolver must not add them based on name presence alone.

The CBN snapshot proves listing only; it does not prove a customer account product. Direct official account-product evidence is still required for PremiumTrust Bank, First Bank Nigeria, Nova Commercial Bank, Standard Chartered Bank Nigeria, and Titan Trust Bank in this audit. 9PSB's official FAQ does establish a phone-number account and NUBAN-standard statement, but its transfer code and exact prefix were not published in the reviewed pages and remain unknown.

## Boundary

The official Hope and SmartCash pages document phone-number or wallet-based transfer behavior. They do not establish an interbank account number format, NUBAN prefix, or bank transfer code. Those fields remain separate and unknown until independently sourced.
